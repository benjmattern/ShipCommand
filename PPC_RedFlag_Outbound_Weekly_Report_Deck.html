Executive Conclusions
Final View
Bottom line
  • Large scale: FY26 live outbound volume exceeds 4.5B shipments and $20B revenue.
  • Material exception exposure: additional postage totals $500.9M.
  • Largest driver: census and unmanifested activity, especially weight-related causes.
  • High leverage: a small number of mailers and a few root causes dominate the risk profile.
  • Monitoring priority: use weekly/monthly trend data to react before the risk compounds.
Suggested management focus
  • Prioritize top mailers by exception value
  • Focus corrective action on weight and unmanifested root causes
  • Track exceptions as % of total revenue, not only raw dollars
  • Review underlying mail class mix before making process changes
  • Use assessment backlog trends as an early warning signal
Summary statement This report supports a clear operational narrative: the business is large and growing, but the cost of red-flag issues is significant, concentrated, and actionable. The highest-value management intervention is to fix the dominant exception drivers in the largest-volume mailers and mail classes.
